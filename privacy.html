Salitreeni – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Salitreeni is a personal, single-user workout log that runs entirely in your own web browser. It has no user accounts and no server of its own.

What data the app handles

Workout sessions, exercises, body measurements and settings that you enter yourself. If you choose to connect an Oura account, the app also reads the following data from the Oura API: daily readiness and sleep scores, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep duration, and workout summaries.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device, in your browser's storage (IndexedDB). It is not uploaded to any server operated by the app's author and it is not shared with any third party. The app uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Oura data

Oura data is requested only after you give explicit consent on Oura's own authorization page, and only for the scopes you approve. Data is fetched directly between your browser and Oura and is kept only on your device. You can disconnect the integration at any time from the app's Settings, and you can revoke access from your Oura account.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data from the app's Settings ("Delete all data") or by clearing the site's data in your browser. Because data never leaves your device, this removes it completely.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
